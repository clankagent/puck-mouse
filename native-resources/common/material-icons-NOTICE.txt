Puck Mouse contains fifteen outlined icon definitions from the 1.7.3 sources
of org.jetbrains.compose.material material-icons-extended-desktop and
material-icons-core-desktop. Original source archives:
https://repo.maven.apache.org/maven2/org/jetbrains/compose/material/material-icons-extended-desktop/1.7.3/material-icons-extended-desktop-1.7.3-sources.jar
https://repo.maven.apache.org/maven2/org/jetbrains/compose/material/material-icons-core-desktop/1.7.3/material-icons-core-desktop-1.7.3-sources.jar

The source package was changed to dev.puckmouse.icons and a provenance comment
was added. Vector definitions, icon names and copyright/license headers are
preserved without other changes.
